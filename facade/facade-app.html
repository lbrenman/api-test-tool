About Acme Front Office

The experience console for Acme's people and product data.

Acme Front Office is a small, phone-friendly app for looking people up, onboarding new hires, checking on teams and browsing the product catalog. It never talks to Acme's systems of record directly. Every screen calls the Workforce & Catalog Experience API, a facade that your integration platform builds and runs.

One tap here can become several back-office calls: a profile joins the employee, their manager and their reports; a team snapshot pages through a whole department and adds it up. That orchestration lives in the facade, which is what this app is for testing and demoing.

The tabs

  • People: search the directory, open a profile badge, then jump to the manager or a direct report.
  • Hire: onboard someone. Resubmitting the same form reuses its idempotency key, so a retry never creates a duplicate.
  • Teams: every department with its active headcount, and a snapshot of each (payroll only appears when your credentials allow it).
  • Catalog: search products by category, stock, price, tag and sort order.
  • Trace: every request and response this page made, with headers and bodies, for debugging.
  • Settings: the facade's address and your credentials (API key, bearer token, basic or OAuth 2.0 client credentials).

Demo mode or live

With demo mode on, the app answers from built-in sample data, including the facade's error cases, so you can try everything before a facade exists. Turn it off in Settings and enter your facade's URL to call it for real. The dot in the header is yellow in demo mode and green when connected to a facade; tap it to open Settings. Tap the Acme Front Office name to come back to the people directory.
